EXTRAsauce Source on GitHub

Your favourite looks, on your new camera.

Extra Sauce rebuilds camera look profiles for bodies they were never made for, so your old Lightroom presets keep working when you upgrade. It also turns phone-app presets into LUTs.

Download for Windows Free · 30 MB · no install
Needs Lightroom Classic or Camera Raw
Camera profiles

One look, every camera

  1. Drop in your .dcp look profiles, or a whole folder of them.
  2. Tick the cameras you shoot with. There are 1,400+ to choose from.
  3. Click Convert. Each look is built for each camera and installed straight into Lightroom.

Converted profiles keep their original names, so your presets find them without any edits.

Phone presets

Rescue a preset you can't export

  1. Save the blank colour chart: 15,625 patches.
  2. Apply the preset to it on your phone and export it at full size.
  3. Drop it back in to get a 33-point .cube LUT.

Tested against an app's own export on a real photo, it comes out at 1.0 ΔE on average.

Accuracy
~1ΔE

That's the average difference from a film pack's own profiles for the same cameras, across 40,000 colours.

How it works

A camera profile runs in this order: matrices → calibration → look → tone curve. Look makers build on Adobe Standard, so Extra Sauce measures what the look does on top of Adobe Standard's calibration:

T = LookTable ∘ HueSatMap ∘ (Adobe Standard HueSatMap)⁻¹

That transform is then placed on your new camera's own Adobe Standard profile, along with the look's tone curve. Each conversion checks itself by rebuilding the look for its original camera and measuring the difference.
